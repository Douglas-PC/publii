Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

This document outlines the privacy policy for Douglas PC. It describes what information we collect, how it is used, and how you can manage your data when using our technology services.

1. Information We Collect

Douglas PC collects minimal data necessary to provide our technology services effectively. This includes contact information when you reach out for support, technical details about your systems during service calls, and basic usage analytics from our website. We do not collect or store any personal files, passwords, or sensitive business data from your devices without explicit permission.

For specific services (e.g., remote support sessions), we may temporarily access system information like hardware specifications or error logs, but this information is not stored long-term and is used solely for troubleshooting purposes.

2. How We Use the Information

The data we collect is used solely to provide and improve our technology services. Contact information is used to communicate with clients about service requests and follow-ups. Technical information is used to diagnose and resolve technology issues. Website analytics help us improve our online presence and service offerings. Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties without your explicit consent.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure. All client information is stored securely with restricted access. During remote support sessions, we use encrypted connections and industry-standard security protocols. We maintain cybersecurity best practices as part of our core business focus.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data from our systems at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us directly through our support channels. We will respond to your request promptly and delete all data associated with your account upon request, subject to any legal retention requirements.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
