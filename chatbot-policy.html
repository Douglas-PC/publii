Chatbot Policy

This page outlines the policy for chatbot usage, data handling, and user rights.

1. Purpose of the Chatbot

The chatbot is designed to assist users by providing automated responses, information, and support. It aims to enhance user experience and streamline communication.

2. Data Collection

The chatbot collects only the data necessary to function, such as user IDs and interaction logs. No personal messages or sensitive information are stored or shared.

3. Data Usage

Collected data is used exclusively to improve chatbot performance and user experience. Data is not sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties.

4. User Rights

Users may request deletion of their data at any time by contacting support. All associated data will be removed upon request.

5. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be posted on this page, and users are encouraged to review it regularly.
